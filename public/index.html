01 / ABOUT

A little about me.

Good logistics isn't just about moving packages. It's about keeping everyone on the same page.

I'm Fangting Chen, but you can call me Jenny. Over the past several years, I've worked across cross-border e-commerce logistics, third-party fulfilment and customer operations — connecting the dots between customers, warehouses, couriers and internal teams.

My work has involved tracking shipments, investigating delivery exceptions, organising collections and making sure operational information stays accurate. I enjoy the practical, detail-focused side of getting things done.

Now based in Auckland, I'm looking to bring that experience to a New Zealand logistics, supply chain or operations team, and I'm also open to remote opportunities in international order operations.
